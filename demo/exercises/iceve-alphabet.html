Ɔkambɛlɛ wʉmba wʉŋgwa acɛla ayɛ:

“m”, “n”, “ŋ”, wʉsaŋgɛ ese:

mb – acɛmbɛ, ɔnʉmbʉsʉ, itimbe.

mgb – ahamgba, mɛmgbasa.

nd – agund, ɛnda, ɛkand.

ny – onyuŋgulu, ɛnyɛgh, ɛnyam.

nz – ɔfanzʉ, ɛnzɔ, nzuwe.

ŋg – atɔŋga, onyuŋgulu, ɛŋgwan.

ŋw – aŋwa, ŋwa, ŋwaghɛ.
